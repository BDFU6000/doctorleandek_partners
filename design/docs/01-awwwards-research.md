# Awwwards Research: Health Platform Sites

Researched 2026-10-01 with the same process as the Tamayoz Al-Da'em site. Zapier (AI by Zapier, "Extract Content From URL") pulled the
award list from `awwwards.com/websites/health/`. "Search Content Across URLs" then read the shortlisted sites, and Chrome
screenshots captured the visuals. The goal is reusable **patterns**. Nothing here is copied as a layout.

Shortlist (from the Awwwards health category, newest first): HEVA Health (Site of the Day), Bevel Health (Honorable Mention 2026),
Photon Health (prescription marketplace, the closest product to ours), Function Health (Honorable Mention), Institute of Health,
Atomic Health.

---

## 1. HEVA Health (Site of the Day)
https://www.hevahealth.com/

| | |
|---|---|
| Layout | Full-bleed photo hero, copy bottom left. Navigation is a **floating rounded capsule** inset from the viewport edges, not a full-width bar. |
| Type | Light condensed serif display, calm sans body, tiny tracked eyebrows ("MEET THE CLINICAL TEAM"). |
| Pattern worth taking | **Two path cards** ("Not sure where to start?" / "Know what you're looking for?"). Each card has a headline, one line of what you get, a "Best if…" line, a small availability footnote, and a full-width button at the bottom. Cards are separate rounded panels with real gaps between them. |

## 2. Bevel Health (Honorable Mention 2026)
https://www.bevel.health/

| | |

| Pattern worth taking | Proof as a quiet **"works with" row** and a centred big claim ("Join over 2.5 million members"). The proof is set in type, not in boxes. |

## 3. Photon Health (prescription marketplace)
https://www.photon.health/

| | |
|---|---|
| Layout | Floating white nav capsule with a cyan "Get Started" pill. Large left-aligned display statement, then a two-up grid of feature cards. |
| Pattern worth taking | **Tag chips** at the top of each card (a dot plus a short tracked word: "CHOICE", "FEWER TRANSFERS"). They tell you what the card is about before you read it. Cards are large rounded panels; the visual sits flush to the card's bottom edge. |

## 4. Function Health (Honorable Mention)
https://www.functionhealth.com

| | |

## 5. Institute of Health, Atomic Health
Text-only reads through Zapier. Both open with a short promise, then a **"what makes us different"** block of three or four claims, each claim a
headline plus one supporting line. That is the shape our Benefits section already has.

---

## Patterns shared by most of the shortlist

1. **Floating, inset navigation capsule.** Four of the five sites detach the nav from the viewport edges and round it.
2. **Separate rounded cards with real gaps**, not a hairline table, for anything the visitor chooses between (paths, roles, features).
3. **Tag chips** on cards to state the topic before the copy.
4. **One accent phrase per headline**, coloured, never a whole coloured line.
5. **Proof in a hairline strip** at the bottom of the hero.
6. **The final CTA as a contained panel**, not a loose block of centred text.

What none of them do: emoji icons, more than one accent colour, rainbow gradients.
